# **App Name**: AZMERA

## Core Features:

- Product Listings: Allow farmers to easily list their produce, including descriptions, images, and pricing.
- Browse and Filter: Enable buyers to browse listings, filter by product type, location, and other criteria.
- Direct Communication: Facilitate direct communication between farmers and buyers regarding orders and product details.
- Dynamic Pricing: AI-powered suggestions providing farmers with optimal pricing strategies based on market trends, seasonality, and product quality. This feature uses a pricing suggestion tool.
- Crop Marketplace: List and buy farm products directly with transparent pricing and secure transactions.
- AI Advisor: Upload images to diagnose pests/diseases and receive AI-backed or expert-linked solutions.
- Transport Coordination: Book or offer delivery with driver authentication (license, ownership proof).
- Gamified Learning: Access step-by-step training and earn rewards for engagement.
- IoT & Weather Integration: Smart alerts for irrigation, storage needs, and weather risks.       
- Escrow Wallet System: Ensures funds are only released upon mutual delivery confirmation. 

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Earthy green (#4CAF50) to evoke nature and freshness.
- Secondary color: Light beige (#F5F5DC) for a clean and natural background.
- Accent: Terracotta (#E07A5F) for highlights and call-to-action buttons.
- Clean and readable typography for product descriptions and communication.
- Simple and intuitive icons to represent product categories and farm features.
- Clean and intuitive layout to facilitate browsing and purchasing.
- Primary: 🌿 #4CAF50 (Green) – growth, agriculture, trust
- Accent: 🔷 #2196F3 (Blue) – tech, clarity, information
- Secondary: ⚪ #FAFAFA (White) – clean background
- Alert: 🔶 #FFC107 (Amber) – warnings, prompts
- Headings: Poppins or Montserrat, bold, 24–32px
- Body Text: Roboto or Open Sans, regular, 14–16px
- Consistent font across pages for professionalism
- Use line icons for AI, transport, marketplace, etc.
- High-contrast, descriptive images with alt text
- Icons should be SVG or PNG with a uniform style
- Mobile-first, responsive design
- Cards, tabs, and modals for organization
- Sticky top nav bar with dropdown menus
- Alt-text for all images
- Color contrast checked for readability
- Keyboard and screen-reader friendly navigation
- Clear, supportive, empowering
- Use culturally relevant terminology and visuals
- Avoid overly technical language in user-facing copy
